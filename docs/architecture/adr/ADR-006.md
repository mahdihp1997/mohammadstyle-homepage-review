# ADR-006 — Versioned policies with deterministic conflict handling

Status: **PROPOSED**, pending Phase 03A review. Date: 2026-10-29.

## Context and decision

Separate definition/version/assignment/resolution/acceptance. Explicit precedence and homogeneous per-item result, otherwise conflict or permissioned whole-booking override.

## Alternatives and trade-off

Taking the strictest rule or arbitrary UUID tie-break silently invents business terms.

## Consequences and revisit trigger

Legal/owner approval required. Accepted artifacts remain immutable; retirement affects future resolution only.

No implementation or deployment is authorized by this ADR. Revisit on approved business requirements, measured load, gateway capabilities or a demonstrated invariant failure; record a superseding ADR rather than silently rewriting accepted decisions.


Hardening clarification: homogeneous-policy/conflict handling is a temporary engineering guard. Owner aggregation strategy remains undecided; alternatives/extension requirements are ADR018 and MULTI_SERVICE_POLICY_OPTIONS.md.
