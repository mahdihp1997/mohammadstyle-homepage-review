# ADR-005 — Immutable quotes, short holds and revision swaps

Status: **PROPOSED**, pending Phase 03A review. Date: 2026-10-29.

## Context and decision

Immutable quote/items/terms; durable hold/expiry; locked whole-plan admission; immutable booking revisions with atomic same-terms reschedule swap.

## Alternatives and trade-off

Redis-only holds and silent mutation of accepted bookings lose recovery and contractual evidence.

## Consequences and revisit trigger

Paid financial reschedule/overlapping candidate self-holds require an additional approved saga. Reaper/admission/confirmation share lock order.

No implementation or deployment is authorized by this ADR. Revisit on approved business requirements, measured load, gateway capabilities or a demonstrated invariant failure; record a superseding ADR rather than silently rewriting accepted decisions.
