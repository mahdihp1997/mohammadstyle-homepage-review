# ADR-010 — Private-first media and evidence-based publication

Status: **PROPOSED**, pending Phase 03A review. Date: 2026-10-29.

## Context and decision

Quarantine, validate/re-encode to immutable derivative keys, per-subject scoped consent and revocation/unpublish pipeline.

## Alternatives and trade-off

A supplied client photo, MIME header or presigned upload URL is not publication permission or validated content.

## Consequences and revisit trigger

Already downloaded copies cannot be recalled. Object/CDN/backup retention and legal rights need owner review.

No implementation or deployment is authorized by this ADR. Revisit on approved business requirements, measured load, gateway capabilities or a demonstrated invariant failure; record a superseding ADR rather than silently rewriting accepted decisions.
