# ADR-018 — Owner decision required for mixed service policies

Status: PROPOSED; Phase03A hardening2026-10-29. Architecture approval pending.

## Decision

Retain explicit POLICY_CONFLICT for heterogeneous winners until owner chooses unified terms, per-item allocations, grouped bookings or a permanent combination restriction.

## Alternatives

Choosing strictest/first/majority policy invents business terms; summing deposits alone does not define refund/capture allocation.

## Consequences

Current72-table envelope is not complete for per-item/grouped extensions. No aggregation strategy selected by engineering.

No03B implementation is authorized by this record.
