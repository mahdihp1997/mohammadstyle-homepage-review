# ADR-008 — Transactional outbox and idempotent consumers

Status: **PROPOSED**, pending Phase 03A review. Date: 2026-10-29.

## Context and decision

Business state/outbox commit together; Redis/BullMQ jobs recover from durable records; inbox/dedup/attempt keys tolerate at-least-once work.

## Alternatives and trade-off

Calling providers inside DB locks or assuming exactly-once queue delivery couples external failures to capacity.

## Consequences and revisit trigger

Unknown external outcomes need reconciliation; provider idempotency capabilities must be verified per adapter.

No implementation or deployment is authorized by this ADR. Revisit on approved business requirements, measured load, gateway capabilities or a demonstrated invariant failure; record a superseding ADR rather than silently rewriting accepted decisions.
