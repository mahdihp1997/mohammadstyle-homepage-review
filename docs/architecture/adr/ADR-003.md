# ADR-003 — Instants, local rules and bounded intervals

Status: **PROPOSED**, pending Phase 03A review. Date: 2026-10-29.

## Context and decision

Persist appointment instants in timestamptz, retain IANA branch timezone, local weekly rules and materialized global staff shifts.

## Alternatives and trade-off

Formatted Jalali strings or fixed Tehran UTC offsets cannot be authoritative scheduling keys.

## Consequences and revisit trigger

DST/travel/overnight handling must be reviewed; ambiguous/nonexistent local times fail closed until explicitly resolved.

No implementation or deployment is authorized by this ADR. Revisit on approved business requirements, measured load, gateway capabilities or a demonstrated invariant failure; record a superseding ADR rather than silently rewriting accepted decisions.
