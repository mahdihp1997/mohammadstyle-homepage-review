# ADR-011 — One provider now, whole-plan service segments later

Status: **PROPOSED**, pending Phase 03A review. Date: 2026-10-29.

## Context and decision

Keep one full-service provider/sequential MVP; represent ordered segments/resource joins and later dependency edges; coalesce same-resource intervals.

## Alternatives and trade-off

Independent per-service slots or union eligibility can produce an infeasible or partially booked plan.

## Consequences and revisit trigger

Parallel/handoff/customer-occupancy/buffer rules must be approved before enabling a multi-provider planner.

No implementation or deployment is authorized by this ADR. Revisit on approved business requirements, measured load, gateway capabilities or a demonstrated invariant failure; record a superseding ADR rather than silently rewriting accepted decisions.
