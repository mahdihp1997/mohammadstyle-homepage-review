# Optional store — implementation contract

The owner requested the complete future store during manual-payment work, with an administrative enable/disable switch. This extends the delivery scope; it does not replace Booking or its capacity engine. The store starts OFF, with no invented products, prices, stock, delivery fees or commercial terms.

The first store supports one owner-selected fulfillment branch, physical products, exact integer Rial prices, a browser cart, server-priced quotes, inventory reservations, verified customer checkout, payment evidence, customer order history, scoped administration and manual fulfillment. Owner-configured pickup and/or flat-fee delivery are supported. There is no assumed courier integration, delivery promise, tax exemption, free shipping, discount, deposit or return entitlement. Final prices, sale/return/shipping text and supported methods must be explicitly configured before activation. Payment is full prepayment only after the owner selects that supported mode; no payment mode is silently defaulted.

Disabling the store hides public shopping navigation and blocks new quotes/checkouts on the server. Existing order access, immutable accepted terms, financial evidence and authorized fulfillment/reconciliation remain available. It never deletes orders or forgets received money.

Products are separate from the six salon services. Original photographs/fonts/references never enter the store publication pipeline. Product/editorial images reuse the approved Portfolio CMS rights checks; revoked rights remove public image delivery.

Quotes freeze current product prices, quantities, catalog/configuration versions, delivery cost and accepted terms. Content hashes identify content, while independent quote UUIDs and actor-scoped idempotency keys identify requests. Checkout revalidates the quote, current configuration and stock under the existing canonical business lock. No browser price is authoritative. Reservations have a fixed configured expiry and are never extended by receipt submission. Competing checkouts cannot oversell. Paid settlement deducts stock once; late or mismatched money opens financial review and never revives an expired reservation.

Booking and product orders share the existing intent/receipt/refund ledger through mutually exclusive typed targets. Orders do not manufacture Booking rows, services, appointment times or capacity allocations. Manual receipts retain the same private encrypted object storage, fresh-MFA bank attestation and unique destination/transaction reconciliation as Booking. Provider-verified and manually reconciled evidence remain distinct. Store adapters must pass all Booking regression tests before delivery.

Customer cancellation of an unpaid order releases its reservation. Paid cancellation is a request for explicit staff review; it cannot fabricate a refund. Fulfillment pauses while a cancellation request is unresolved. Restocking requires explicit staff confirmation that goods remain with the salon or have been received back and are suitable for stock. Financial review remains bounded by actual received funds. No real payment, SMS or public deployment is authorized by this implementation task.
