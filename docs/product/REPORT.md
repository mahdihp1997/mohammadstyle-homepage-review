## Visual and sanitization decisions

The approved charcoal/champagne layout, composition and Booking controls remain. Product/public packages use system fonts and approved native brand artwork in places where internal photographs cannot legally be published. There is no fictional before/after comparison or stock-client substitute. The public Homepage gallery reads only CMS-approved media. Private-review images/fonts still render under the explicit internal dev profile; those binaries remain in the original private source and are physically excluded from deployment packages. The mark is editable SVG geometry traced from the owner-approved isolated brand mark, not the entire identity board. Public indexing stays off until the owner accepts the production origin.

The sanitized review preserves existing public placeholder files, system fonts and review disclosure. It omits original client photographs, private fonts/references, credentials, test databases/dumps, internal face-containing screenshots, local certificate keys and unnecessary development artifacts. Its private-font route is unavailable. Backend/Booking logic, SQL contracts/state/validation/calculation/calendar and new tests are identical to the private implementation. Two pre-existing public test adaptations (system-font/review-disclosure assertions and the configurable no-JavaScript baseURL) remain unchanged from the public base; see EXPORT.md. These review-only presentation differences do not change Booking behavior; they do mean the sanitized preview is not the original photographic visual approval artifact.

Owner MFA cannot be disabled. Within an affected branch, scheduling changes remain conservative while live allocations exist; unrelated branches are usable. These choices preserve accepted data rather than introduce unapproved commercial rules.
